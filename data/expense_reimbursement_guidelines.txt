NovaTech Solutions — Expense Reimbursement Guidelines
Document ID: FIN-EXP-2026-003 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version 2.4
Classification: Internal — All Employees

================================================================================
1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

This document defines eligible expenses, submission procedures, and reimbursement
timelines for NovaTech Solutions employees. All expenses must be business-related,
reasonable, and supported by itemized receipts.

Expense system: ExpenseBot (expensebot.novatech.io)
Corporate card: Brex (issued to Level 3+ or with manager approval)
Questions: finance-expenses@novatech.io

================================================================================
2. GENERAL RULES
================================================================================

2.1 Submission Deadline
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurring. Expenses older than 60
days require Finance VP approval. Expenses older than 90 days will not be reimbursed.

2.2 Receipt Requirements
  - Required for all expenses $25 and above
  - Itemized receipts required (credit card summaries not accepted)
  - Foreign receipts: attach English translation if not in English
  - Digital receipts accepted (PDF, photo). Must show vendor, date, amount, items

2.3 Approval Workflow
  Under $500: Manager approval
  $500 – $2,000: Manager + Department head approval
  Over $2,000: Manager + Finance approval
  Over $10,000: Pre-approval required before purchase (email finance@novatech.io)

2.4 Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses reimbursed within 5 business days via direct deposit (added to
next payroll cycle). Brex card expenses auto-reconciled monthly.

2.5 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
  - Personal travel, meals, or entertainment
  - Traffic violations, parking tickets, toll evasion fines
  - Alcoholic beverages (except approved client dinners — see 4.3)
  - Gym memberships (use wellness stipend instead)
  - Commuting costs to NovaTech office (except approved relocation)
  - Spouse/partner travel unless pre-approved for company events
  - First-class airfare (business class permitted for flights over 6 hours)
  - Personal mobile phone plans (company phone provided for Level 4+)

================================================================================
3. TRAVEL EXPENSES
================================================================================

3.1 Airfare
  - Economy class default for flights under 6 hours
  - Premium economy permitted for flights 6–10 hours
  - Business class permitted for flights over 10 hours or red-eye international
  - Book through TravelPortal (Concur) for preferred rates and auto-policy check
  - Personal loyalty points: employee may keep, but must book cheapest available
    NovaTech-eligible fare

3.2 Ground Transportation
  - Rideshare (Uber/Lyft): approved for business travel
  - Rental cars: mid-size standard, insurance via corporate account (decline counter
    insurance). GPS included. Fuel reimbursable with receipt.
  - Personal vehicle: $0.655/mile (2026 IRS rate) for business miles only
  - Parking: reimbursed with receipt, max $50/day in major cities

3.3 Lodging
  Per-night limits (before tax):
  Tier 1 cities (SF, NYC, London, Tokyo): $350/night
  Tier 2 cities (Austin, Seattle, Chicago, Berlin): $250/night
  Tier 3 cities (all others): $175/night
  Extended stay (7+ nights): consider Airbnb or extended-stay hotel with Finance
  pre-approval. Incidentals (minibar, movies, spa) not reimbursable.

3.4 Meals During Travel
  Per diem (when itemized receipts impractical):
  Breakfast: $20 | Lunch: $25 | Dinner: $45
  Per diem not combinable with actual meal reimbursement for same meal.
  Travel day meals fully reimbursable. Non-travel day meals follow Section 4.

3.5 International Travel
  - Register travel in International SOS portal before departure
  - Travel insurance provided automatically via corporate policy
  - Visa fees reimbursable with receipt
  - Currency conversion: use Brex rate or Oanda daily rate for cash expenses
  - High-risk destinations require Security and HR pre-approval

================================================================================
4. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
================================================================================

4.1 Business Meals (Internal)
Team meals during working hours: $30/person limit. Requires at least 2 employees.
Monthly team lunch budget: $50/person (managers track, submit monthly).

4.2 Business Meals (External)
Client or partner meals: $75/person limit. At least one NovaTech employee and one
external guest required. Document attendees and business purpose in ExpenseBot.

4.3 Client Entertainment
Pre-approval required for expenses over $150/person. Alcohol permitted at client
dinners with director approval. Sporting events and concerts require VP approval
and must include at least one client/prospect.

4.4 Team Events
Quarterly team events: $100/person budget. Submit as group expense with attendee
list. Holiday party budget managed by People Ops (not individual expense).

================================================================================
5. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
================================================================================

5.1 Learning Budget
Annual professional development budget by level:
  Level 1–2: $1,500/year
  Level 3–4: $2,500/year
  Level 5+: $4,000/year

Covers: conferences, courses, certifications, books, professional memberships.
Submit pre-approval for expenses over $500. Conference attendance requires manager
approval and must share learnings with team upon return.

5.2 Approved Conferences (2026 Fast-Track)
Pre-approved (no additional approval needed):
  - AWS re:Invent, Google Cloud Next, Microsoft Build
  - KubeCon, PyCon, React Conf, Strange Loop
  - SaaStr, Dreamforce (sales roles only)

5.3 Certifications
Company-sponsored certifications (exam fees reimbursed):
  - AWS Solutions Architect, GCP Professional, Azure Administrator
  - CISSP, CISM (Security team)
  - PMP, Scrum Master (PM team)
  - CKA, CKAD (Engineering)

Passing bonus: $500 for first attempt success. One retake fee covered.

================================================================================
6. HOME OFFICE AND REMOTE EXPENSES
================================================================================

6.1 Internet Stipend
See Remote Work Policy (HR-RWP-2026-004):
  Fully remote: $75/month
  Hybrid: $40/month

6.2 Home Office Setup
One-time $1,000 stipend for eligible new remote hires (see Remote Work Policy).

6.3 Equipment Purchases
Monitors, keyboards, mice: purchase through IT catalog (preferred) or reimburse
up to $800 total for dual monitor setup. Ergonomic equipment (standing desk,
chair): submit through HR ergonomics program, up to $500 with occupational health
recommendation.

6.4 Co-Working
Up to $300/month with manager pre-approval. WeWork/Regus preferred vendors.

================================================================================
7. SOFTWARE AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

7.1 Company-Provided Tools
Standard tools (Slack, GitHub, Jira, Figma, Notion) provided by IT. Do not
purchase separately.

7.2 Individual Software
Role-specific software may be reimbursed up to $30/month with manager approval.
Examples: Raycast Pro, Alfred Powerpack, iTerm subscription. Submit via ExpenseBot
under "Software — Individual Productivity."

7.3 Prohibited
Personal streaming services, gaming subscriptions, personal cloud storage.

================================================================================
8. CLIENT PROJECT EXPENSES
================================================================================

8.1 Billable Expenses
Client-related expenses for billable projects must be pre-approved by Project
Manager and tracked with client code in ExpenseBot. Mark as "Billable" for
invoicing.

8.2 On-Site Client Work
When working at client site for 5+ consecutive days:
  - Per diem: $60/day (covers meals and incidentals)
  - Client-provided meals: deduct from per diem
  - Extended client site: discuss housing with Project Manager

================================================================================
9. EXPENSE REPORTING PROCEDURES
================================================================================

9.1 ExpenseBot Workflow
  1. Log in via Okta SSO
  2. Create new report (select correct cost center)
  3. Add expenses with receipt attachments
  4. Submit for approval
  5. Track status in "My Reports" dashboard

9.2 Cost Centers
  Engineering: CC-1000
  Product: CC-2000
  Sales: CC-3000
  Marketing: CC-4000
  G&A: CC-5000
  Customer Success: CC-6000

9.3 Tax Compliance
NovaTech does not gross-up reimbursements for tax purposes. Employees responsible
for personal tax implications. VAT receipts required for EU expenses (NovaTech
VAT ID: EU372019847).

9.4 Audit
Finance audits 10% of expense reports monthly. Fraudulent submissions result in
termination and legal action. Common audit flags:
  - Duplicate receipts
  - Round-number amounts without receipts
  - Weekend expenses without business justification
  - Expenses near policy limits consistently

================================================================================
10. SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES
================================================================================

10.1 Relocation
Relocation package for eligible hires (Level 3+, approved by HR):
  - Moving expenses up to $5,000 (receipts required)
  - Temporary housing up to 30 days, $200/night
  - House-hunting trip: 3 days, standard travel rates

10.2 Emergency Expenses
Unexpected business expenses (equipment failure during travel, emergency client
visit): contact manager immediately. Retroactive approval within 48 hours.
Emergency travel booking: call TravelPortal 24/7 line.

10.3 Charitable Contributions
NovaTech corporate matching: employee donations to approved 501(c)(3) organizations
matched up to $1,000/year. Submit through Benevity portal (not ExpenseBot).
