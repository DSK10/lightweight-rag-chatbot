NovaTech Solutions — Code of Conduct
Document ID: LEG-COC-2026-001 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version 3.0
Classification: Internal — All Employees, Contractors, Board Members

================================================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

NovaTech Solutions is committed to the highest standards of ethical conduct. This
Code of Conduct applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and board
members ("personnel") regardless of location or role.

Every person at NovaTech is responsible for understanding and following this Code.
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination and
referral to law enforcement.

Ethics Hotline (anonymous): 1-800-555-0199 | ethics@novatech.io
General Counsel: legal@novatech.io

================================================================================
2. OUR COMMITMENTS
================================================================================

2.1 Integrity
We are honest and transparent in all business dealings. We do not misrepresent
products, services, financial results, or our capabilities. Marketing claims must
be verifiable and approved by Legal and Product Marketing.

2.2 Respect
We treat colleagues, customers, partners, and community members with dignity
and respect. We value diverse perspectives and create an environment where everyone
can contribute their best work.

2.3 Accountability
We take ownership of our decisions and their consequences. We admit mistakes,
learn from them, and implement corrective actions. We do not blame others or
hide problems.

2.4 Compliance
We comply with all applicable laws and regulations in every jurisdiction where
we operate, including anti-corruption, antitrust, export control, privacy, and
employment laws.

================================================================================
3. WORKPLACE BEHAVIOR
================================================================================

3.1 Anti-Discrimination and Harassment
NovaTech prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual
orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status,
genetic information, or any other protected characteristic.

Harassment includes unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics that
creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment. Examples:
  - Offensive jokes, slurs, or epithets
  - Unwelcome physical contact or sexual advances
  - Displaying offensive images or materials
  - Threatening or bullying behavior
  - Retaliation against anyone who reports concerns

Report to: HR (hr@novatech.io), your manager, or Ethics Hotline. All reports
investigated promptly and confidentially to the extent possible.

3.2 Violence and Threats
Violence, threats of violence, and intimidating behavior are prohibited. This
includes verbal threats, physical altercations, and bringing weapons to company
premises (except where permitted by law).

3.3 Substance Abuse
Working while impaired by alcohol or illegal drugs is prohibited. Prescription
medications that impair work ability should be discussed with HR for reasonable
accommodations. Alcohol at company events must be consumed responsibly.

3.4 Workplace Relationships
Romantic relationships between employees where one has direct or indirect authority
over the other must be disclosed to HR. HR will implement conflict mitigation
(meeting reassignment, reporting line change). Failure to disclose may result in
termination of one or both parties.

3.5 Social Media
When posting about NovaTech on personal social media:
  - Clearly state opinions are your own (not company positions)
  - Do not share confidential or proprietary information
  - Do not disparage customers, competitors, or colleagues
  - Do not post content that could harm NovaTech's reputation
  - Official company social media posts require Marketing approval

================================================================================
4. CUSTOMER AND PARTNER RELATIONS
================================================================================

4.1 Fair Dealing
We compete fairly and honestly. We do not engage in:
  - False or misleading statements about competitors
  - Industrial espionage or theft of trade secrets
  - Price fixing, bid rigging, or market allocation (antitrust violations)
  - Bribery or kickbacks (see Section 6)

4.2 Customer Commitments
We deliver products and services as promised. We communicate honestly about
capabilities, limitations, timelines, and pricing. We honor SLAs and contracts.
Customer data is protected per our security and privacy policies.

4.3 Gifts and Hospitality
  - Employees may accept gifts valued at $75 or less
  - Gifts over $75 must be declined or donated to charity
  - Entertainment (meals, events) must be reasonable and business-related
  - Never accept cash or cash equivalents
  - All gifts from vendors/customers over $25 must be reported in Gift Registry
    (ethics.novatech.io/gifts)

4.4 Conflicts of Interest
Avoid situations where personal interests conflict with NovaTech's interests.
Disclose potential conflicts to HR and your manager. Examples requiring disclosure:
  - Financial interest in a competitor, vendor, or customer
  - Outside employment or consulting
  - Board membership at another company
  - Family member working at a competitor or key vendor
  - Personal relationship with a vendor decision-maker

Annual conflict of interest disclosure required for Level 4+ and all managers.

================================================================================
5. PROTECTING COMPANY ASSETS
================================================================================

5.1 Intellectual Property
All work product created during employment belongs to NovaTech. This includes
code, designs, documentation, inventions, and processes. Do not use NovaTech IP
for personal projects without written approval from Legal.

5.2 Confidential Information
Protect confidential information at all times. Do not discuss internal matters
with unauthorized persons. NDAs required before sharing confidential information
externally. Confidential obligations continue after employment ends.

5.3 Equipment and Resources
Use company equipment and resources for business purposes. Limited personal use
permitted (checking personal email during breaks) but must not interfere with
work or violate policies. Do not use company resources for:
  - Personal business ventures
  - Political campaigning
  - Cryptocurrency mining
  - Accessing inappropriate content

5.4 Data Protection
Handle customer and employee data per Information Security Policy (SEC-ISP-2026-002).
Report data breaches immediately. Do not access data beyond your authorized scope.

================================================================================
6. ANTI-CORRUPTION AND BRIBERY
================================================================================

6.1 Zero Tolerance
NovaTech prohibits bribery and corruption in all forms, including:
  - Cash payments to government officials
  - Excessive gifts or hospitality to win business
  - Facilitation payments ("speed money")
  - Charitable donations intended to influence decisions

6.2 Government Officials
Special rules apply when dealing with government employees or officials:
  - No gifts over $25 without Legal pre-approval
  - All meetings documented with business purpose
  - Government contract bids must be transparent and fair
  - Export control compliance mandatory (see 6.4)

6.3 Third-Party Agents
Due diligence required before engaging distributors, resellers, or consultants
who interact with government officials on NovaTech's behalf. Anti-corruption
clauses required in all third-party agreements.

6.4 Export Controls
NovaTech products and technology subject to US export control laws (EAR, ITAR).
Do not export, re-export, or transfer technology to sanctioned countries or
entities without Legal approval. Sanctioned countries (2026): Cuba, Iran, North
Korea, Syria, Crimea region. Employee travel to these regions prohibited.

================================================================================
7. FINANCIAL INTEGRITY
================================================================================

7.1 Accurate Records
All financial records, expense reports, time sheets, and business documents must
be accurate and complete. Falsifying records is grounds for immediate termination.

7.2 Internal Controls
Follow financial policies and approval workflows. Do not circumvent controls.
Segregation of duties must be maintained. Report control weaknesses to Internal
Audit (audit@novatech.io).

7.3 Insider Trading
Employees with material non-public information about NovaTech or companies we
do business with must not trade securities. Blackout periods apply before
earnings releases (10 days before to 2 days after). Pre-clearance required for
Level 5+ and Finance/Legal employees. Violations reported to SEC.

7.4 Fraud
Report suspected fraud to Ethics Hotline or Internal Audit. NovaTech investigates
all reports. Whistleblowers protected from retaliation per Whistleblower Policy
(LEG-WBP-2025-001).

================================================================================
8. ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY
================================================================================

8.1 Sustainability
NovaTech commits to carbon-neutral operations by 2028. Employees encouraged to:
  - Minimize printing (default to digital)
  - Use video conferencing instead of travel when practical
  - Participate in office recycling and e-waste programs
  - Choose sustainable vendors when options are equivalent

8.2 Community Engagement
NovaTech supports community through:
  - NovaTech Foundation: $500K annual charitable giving
  - Volunteer Time Off: 16 hours/year paid volunteer time
  - STEM education partnerships in Austin, Seattle, and Bangalore
  - Pro-bono technology support for approved nonprofits

8.3 Human Rights
NovaTech supports universal human rights. We do not tolerate forced labor,
child labor, or human trafficking in our operations or supply chain. Supplier
Code of Conduct required for all vendors.

================================================================================
9. REPORTING VIOLATIONS
================================================================================

9.1 How to Report
  - Manager or skip-level manager
  - HR Business Partner
  - Legal/Compliance: ethics@novatech.io
  - Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-0199 (anonymous, 24/7, multilingual)
  - Online portal: ethics.novatech.io/report

9.2 Non-Retaliation
NovaTech strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in
good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation
of this Code and grounds for termination.

9.3 Investigation Process
  - Acknowledgment within 48 hours
  - Investigation completed within 30 days (complex cases: 60 days)
  - Findings communicated to reporter (where identity known)
  - Corrective action taken as appropriate
  - Annual ethics report to Board Audit Committee

9.4 False Reports
Knowingly false or malicious reports may result in disciplinary action. Good-faith
reports that cannot be substantiated are not considered violations.

================================================================================
10. ACKNOWLEDGMENT AND TRAINING
================================================================================

All personnel must:
  - Read and acknowledge this Code within 14 days of hire
  - Complete annual ethics training by March 31 each year
  - Re-acknowledge upon material policy updates
  - Certify annual conflict of interest disclosure (Level 4+ and managers)

Acknowledgment tracked in WorkDay. Failure to complete: access restrictions after
14-day grace period.
