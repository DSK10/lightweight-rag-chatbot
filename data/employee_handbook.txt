NovaTech Solutions — Employee Handbook
Document ID: HR-HB-2026-001 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version 3.2
Classification: Internal — All Employees

================================================================================
1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
================================================================================

NovaTech Solutions is a cloud-native enterprise software company headquartered in
Austin, Texas, with regional offices in Seattle, London, and Bangalore. Founded
in 2014, NovaTech provides AI-powered workflow automation, data integration, and
compliance management platforms to mid-market and enterprise clients in financial
services, healthcare, and retail.

Mission: Empower organizations to automate complex business processes securely
and at scale.

Core Values: Integrity, Customer Obsession, Technical Excellence, Inclusion,
Accountability.

Total Employees (Q1 2026): 1,847 across 12 countries.

================================================================================
2. EMPLOYMENT CLASSIFICATIONS
================================================================================

2.1 Full-Time Employees (FTE)
Standard work week is 40 hours. Eligible for full benefits package including
medical, dental, vision, 401(k) with 4% company match, and equity grants.

2.2 Part-Time Employees
Work 20–29 hours per week. Eligible for prorated PTO and medical benefits if
working 25+ hours.

2.3 Contractors
Engaged through approved vendors only. Contractors are not eligible for NovaTech
benefits, stock options, or internal systems access beyond project scope.
Contractor badge color: ORANGE. Must be escorted in R&D zones (Building C).

2.4 Interns
Summer and co-op programs run May–August and January–April. Intern pay: $38/hr
(engineering), $28/hr (business operations). Housing stipend of $2,000/month
available for relocations over 50 miles.

================================================================================
3. WORK SCHEDULES AND ATTENDANCE
================================================================================

3.1 Core Collaboration Hours
All employees must be available 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM in their local time zone
for meetings and team syncs, unless exempted by their director.

3.2 Flexible Scheduling
Employees may adjust start/end times with manager approval. Four-day work weeks
(10-hour days) are permitted in Engineering and Product with VP approval.

3.3 Attendance Tracking
FTE employees log time in WorkDay. Missing 3 consecutive unreported absences
triggers automatic HR outreach. Chronic tardiness (5+ late arrivals per month)
may result in performance review.

3.4 Overtime
Non-exempt employees receive 1.5x hourly rate for hours exceeding 40/week.
Exempt employees (Level 5+) are not eligible for overtime pay but may receive
comp time with director approval (max 16 hours per quarter).

================================================================================
4. COMPENSATION AND PAY
================================================================================

4.1 Pay Periods
Bi-weekly payroll, deposited every other Friday. Direct deposit is mandatory.

4.2 Salary Bands (2026)
Level 1 (Associate):     $65,000 – $85,000
Level 2 (Specialist):    $85,000 – $110,000
Level 3 (Senior):        $110,000 – $145,000
Level 4 (Staff/Lead):    $145,000 – $185,000
Level 5 (Principal):       $185,000 – $230,000
Level 6 (Director+):       $230,000+

4.3 Annual Reviews
Conducted each November. Merit increases effective January 1. Typical merit
range: 3–8%. Top performers (rated "Exceptional") may receive 10–15% increases
and additional RSU grants.

4.4 Referral Bonus
$5,000 for engineering hires, $3,000 for non-engineering, paid after 90-day
retention of referred employee. Maximum 4 referral bonuses per employee per year.

================================================================================
5. BENEFITS SUMMARY
================================================================================

5.1 Medical Insurance
NovaTech covers 90% of employee premium, 70% of dependent premium. Three plan
tiers: Bronze (HDHP), Silver (PPO), Gold (Premium PPO). Open enrollment: November
1–15 annually.

5.2 Dental and Vision
Fully covered for employees. Delta Dental and VSP standard plans.

5.3 401(k) Retirement
4% company match on first 6% employee contribution. Immediate vesting on match.
Fidelity administers the plan.

5.4 Life and Disability
Basic life insurance: 1x annual salary (company-paid). Supplemental life available.
Short-term disability: 60% salary for up to 12 weeks. Long-term disability:
60% salary after 90-day waiting period.

5.5 Wellness Program
$600 annual wellness stipend for gym memberships, mental health apps, or fitness
equipment. Annual biometric screening on-site (optional, incentivized with $200
HSA contribution).

5.6 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
Free confidential counseling, 8 sessions per issue per year. Available to
employees and household members. Provider: Lyra Health.

================================================================================
6. LEAVE POLICIES (SUMMARY)
================================================================================

Detailed policies are in separate documents. Summary:

- PTO: 15 days (Year 1), 20 days (Year 2–4), 25 days (Year 5+)
- Sick Leave: 10 days/year, separate from PTO
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid (primary), 8 weeks paid (secondary)
- Bereavement: 5 days (immediate family), 3 days (extended family)
- Jury Duty: Paid for duration of service
- Military Leave: Protected per USERRA

================================================================================
7. WORKPLACE CONDUCT
================================================================================

7.1 Anti-Harassment
NovaTech maintains zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation.
Report concerns to HR at hr@novatech.io or the Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-0199.

7.2 Dress Code
Business casual in office. Client-facing roles: business professional on request.
Remote video calls: professional attire from waist up required for external meetings.

7.3 Substance Policy
Illegal drug use prohibited on company premises. Alcohol permitted at approved
company events only. No employee may work while impaired.

7.4 Weapons
Firearms and weapons prohibited on all NovaTech property, including parking
garages, except where prohibited by local law.

================================================================================
8. IT AND EQUIPMENT
================================================================================

8.1 Company Equipment
Laptops issued based on role:
  Engineering: MacBook Pro 16" M3 or Dell XPS 15
  Design: MacBook Pro 14" M3 Max
  General: MacBook Air M3 or Dell Latitude 5540

Monitors: Up to 2 external monitors (27" max) reimbursed up to $800 total.

8.2 Software Licenses
JetBrains, GitHub Enterprise, Figma, and Slack provided. Personal software
installation requires IT approval via ServiceNow ticket.

8.3 Equipment Return
All equipment must be returned within 5 business days of separation. Failure to
return may result in deduction from final paycheck (where legally permitted).

================================================================================
9. SEPARATION AND OFFBOARDING
================================================================================

9.1 Voluntary Resignation
Standard notice: 2 weeks (Level 1–3), 4 weeks (Level 4+). Exit interview
scheduled with HR. COBRA information provided on last day.

9.2 Involuntary Termination
Final paycheck within 72 hours (CA employees) or next pay cycle. Unused PTO paid
out in states where required by law. Access revoked immediately upon notification.

9.3 Garden Leave
Senior employees (Level 5+) may be placed on garden leave during notice period.
Full salary and benefits continue. External employment prohibited during garden leave.
