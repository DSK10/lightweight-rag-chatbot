# NovaTech Solutions — Business Travel Policy

**Document ID:** HR-TRV-2026-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal — All Employees

## Overview

This policy governs business travel for NovaTech employees. All travel must be pre-approved by your manager and booked through the corporate travel portal (travel.novatech.io).

## Pre-Approval

- Domestic trips: manager approval required at least 5 business days before departure.
- International trips: director approval + Legal review for restricted countries.
- Trips over $3,000 estimated cost require Finance pre-approval.

## Booking Guidelines

| Category | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| Flights | Economy class default; business class allowed for flights over 6 hours with VP approval |
| Hotels | Up to $250/night domestic, $350/night international (major metro exceptions apply) |
| Ground transport | Uber/Lyft or rental car via approved vendor; personal vehicle mileage at IRS rate |
| Meals | Per diem: $75/day domestic, $100/day international |

## Expense Submission

Submit expenses within 14 days of return via WorkDay. Attach receipts for all items over $25. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless part of an approved client dinner per Expense Reimbursement Guidelines (FIN-EXP-2026-002).

## Safety

Register international travel with Security (security@novatech.io). Follow Information Security Policy when using devices abroad — use VPN, no public USB charging, report lost devices within 1 hour.
