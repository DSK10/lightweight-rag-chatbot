NovaTech Solutions — Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Document ID: HR-PTO-2026-005 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version 2.0
Classification: Internal — All Employees

================================================================================
1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

NovaTech Solutions provides Paid Time Off (PTO) for rest, personal activities,
and vacation. PTO is a single bank combining vacation and personal days (sick
leave is separate — see Section 5).

PTO accrues based on tenure and is managed in WorkDay. PTO balance visible in
WorkDay self-service portal and mobile app.

Questions: pto@novatech.io | HR Service Center: hr@novatech.io

================================================================================
2. PTO ACCRUAL
================================================================================

2.1 Accrual Rates (Full-Time Employees)
  Year 1 (0–12 months):     15 days/year (1.25 days/month)
  Year 2–4 (13–48 months):  20 days/year (1.67 days/month)
  Year 5+ (49+ months):     25 days/year (2.08 days/month)

2.2 Accrual Schedule
PTO accrues on the 1st of each month. Available for use after completing first
30 days of employment. Accrual begins on start date.

2.3 Part-Time Employees
PTO accrues pro-rata based on hours worked. Minimum 20 hours/week for accrual
eligibility. Formula: (hours worked / 40) × full-time accrual rate.

2.4 Maximum Balance (Cap)
PTO accrual stops when balance reaches 1.5× annual allotment:
  Year 1: cap at 22.5 days
  Year 2–4: cap at 30 days
  Year 5+: cap at 37.5 days

Accrual resumes when balance drops below cap. Encouraged to use PTO regularly.

2.5 Tenure Calculation
Tenure calculated from original hire date. Rehires within 12 months: tenure
restored. Rehires after 12 months: treated as new hire for PTO purposes unless
approved by HR VP.

================================================================================
3. USING PTO
================================================================================

3.1 Request Process
  1. Submit PTO request in WorkDay at least 2 weeks in advance (recommended)
  2. Manager approves/denies within 3 business days
  3. Auto-approved if no manager response within 3 days (except blackouts)
  4. Block calendar in Google Calendar (auto-sync from WorkDay)

3.2 Minimum/Maximum Request
  - Minimum: 2 hours (partial day)
  - Maximum consecutive: 15 business days without VP approval
  - PTO over 15 consecutive days: requires HR and VP approval, 30-day notice

3.3 Same-Day PTO
Permitted with manager notification (Slack/email acceptable). No approval needed
for same-day if balance available, but manager must be informed before start
of workday.

3.4 Blackout Periods
PTO requests may be restricted during:
  - End of quarter (last 5 business days of March, June, September, December)
    for Sales and Customer Success teams
  - Major product launches (announced 60 days in advance)
  - Company all-hands week (first week of January)
  - On-call rotation (SRE team — swap required before PTO)

Blackout exceptions require director approval.

3.5 Team Coverage
Employees responsible for ensuring coverage during PTO. Document handoff in team
wiki or Slack channel. For client-facing roles, notify clients 1 week in advance.

================================================================================
4. PTO PAYOUT AND CARRYOVER
================================================================================

4.1 End of Year
Unused PTO carries over to next calendar year, subject to cap (Section 2.4).
No "use it or lose it" — but employees encouraged to use at least 10 days/year
for wellbeing.

4.2 Separation Payout
Upon termination or resignation:
  - California, Colorado, Illinois, Massachusetts, Montana, Nebraska, North
    Dakota employees: all unused PTO paid out at final rate of pay
  - Other states: payout per state law (typically accrued but unused PTO paid)
  - Payout on final paycheck
  - No payout for employees terminated for cause (theft, violence, data breach)

4.3 Buy-Back Program
No PTO buy-back program currently offered. Employees may not sell or transfer
PTO to colleagues.

4.4 Negative Balance
PTO balance may not go negative. If PTO taken before accrual (not permitted),
employee must repay via payroll deduction or return to work.

================================================================================
5. SICK LEAVE
================================================================================

5.1 Allotment
10 sick days per calendar year, separate from PTO. Does not carry over (resets
January 1). Does not pay out on separation (except where required by state law).

5.2 Eligible Uses
  - Employee illness or injury
  - Medical appointments
  - Care for ill family member (spouse, child, parent)
  - Mental health days (no documentation required for 1–3 consecutive days)

5.3 Documentation
Sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires doctor's note. HR may request
documentation for patterns of sick leave use (e.g., every Monday).

5.4 State-Specific Sick Leave
Employees in states with mandatory sick leave laws (CA, NY, WA, etc.) receive
the greater of NovaTech sick leave or state minimum.

================================================================================
6. PARENTAL AND FAMILY LEAVE
================================================================================

6.1 Parental Leave
  Primary caregiver: 16 weeks fully paid
  Secondary caregiver: 8 weeks fully paid
  Applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement
  Must be taken within 12 months of event
  May be taken consecutively or intermittently (with manager approval)
  Benefits continue during leave

6.2 Pregnancy Leave
In addition to parental leave, pregnant employees receive reasonable accommodations
and additional medical leave per state law (e.g., CA Pregnancy Disability Leave:
up to 4 months).

6.3 Family Care Leave
Up to 2 weeks unpaid leave per year for family care (extended family illness,
eldercare). May use PTO or take unpaid. FMLA eligibility: 12 weeks unpaid
protected leave for qualifying events (after 12 months employment, 1250 hours).

6.4 Bereavement Leave
  Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days
  Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle): 3 paid days
  Additional unpaid leave available via FMLA if applicable
  Immediate family definition includes domestic partner (registered)

================================================================================
7. COMPANY HOLIDAYS
================================================================================

7.1 Observed Holidays (2026)
NovaTech offices closed on:
  - New Year's Day: January 1
  - Martin Luther King Jr. Day: January 19
  - Presidents' Day: February 16
  - Memorial Day: May 25
  - Juneteenth: June 19
  - Independence Day: July 3 (observed)
  - Labor Day: September 7
  - Thanksgiving: November 26–27
  - Christmas Eve: December 24
  - Christmas Day: December 25
  - Floating Holiday: 1 day (employee choice, manager approval)

Total: 12 company holidays + 1 floating holiday

7.2 Holiday Pay
Full-time employees receive holiday pay (regular rate). Required to work: 1.5x
rate or comp day (employee choice). Part-time: pro-rata if normally scheduled.

7.3 Global Holidays
International employees receive local public holidays per country schedule
(available in WorkDay). UK: 8 bank holidays. India: 10 public holidays.
Germany: 10 public holidays (varies by state).

================================================================================
8. SPECIAL LEAVE TYPES
================================================================================

8.1 Jury Duty
Paid leave for duration of jury service. Submit summons to HR within 5 days.
Employee retains any jury pay received.

8.2 Military Leave
Protected leave per USERRA. Up to 15 days paid military leave per year for
training/drills. Extended active duty: unpaid protected leave, benefits continue
for 30 days.

8.3 Voting Leave
Up to 4 hours paid leave to vote in elections (where not provided by state law
already). Must notify manager 2 days in advance.

8.4 Volunteer Time Off (VTO)
16 hours paid volunteer time per year for approved charitable activities.
Register through Benevity portal. Must be 501(c)(3) organization.

8.5 Sabbatical
Employees with 7+ years tenure may apply for 4–8 week unpaid sabbatical.
Requires VP and HR approval. Benefits continue for first 4 weeks. One sabbatical
per 7-year period. Must be taken in minimum 2-week blocks.

8.6 Leave of Absence (Unpaid)
Extended unpaid leave (beyond FMLA) may be granted for personal reasons with
HR VP approval. Maximum 6 months. Benefits suspended after 30 days (COBRA offered).
Return not guaranteed for leaves over 3 months (position may be filled).

================================================================================
9. PTO AND REMOTE WORK
================================================================================

Remote employees follow the same PTO policy. PTO may be taken from any location.
No requirement to be reachable during PTO (disable Slack notifications).
Emergency contact: provide manager with optional contact method for true emergencies.

International PTO: permitted if employee is in approved remote work country.
Notify HR if PTO exceeds 30 consecutive days (tax implications).

================================================================================
10. POLICY ADMINISTRATION
================================================================================

10.1 Tracking
All PTO managed in WorkDay. Employees responsible for monitoring balance.
Discrepancies: contact HR within 30 days.

10.2 Manager Responsibilities
  - Approve PTO promptly and fairly
  - Ensure team coverage and workload distribution
  - Encourage employees to use PTO (monitor for burnout)
  - Do not deny PTO arbitrarily or retaliate for PTO use
  - Report PTO abuse concerns to HR (do not investigate independently)

10.3 Abuse
PTO abuse (falsifying reasons, patterns of unapproved absence) may result in
disciplinary action. Sick leave patterns reviewed by HR quarterly.
