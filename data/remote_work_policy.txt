NovaTech Solutions — Remote Work Policy
Document ID: HR-RWP-2026-004 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version 2.1
Classification: Internal — All Employees

================================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================

This policy governs remote and hybrid work arrangements for all NovaTech Solutions
employees. NovaTech operates a "Remote-First, Office-Optional" model adopted
company-wide in March 2023.

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors follow
vendor-specific agreements but must comply with Sections 5 (Security) and 6
(Equipment) when accessing NovaTech systems remotely.

================================================================================
2. WORK ARRANGEMENT TYPES
================================================================================

2.1 Fully Remote
Employee works 100% from a home office or approved co-working space. Must reside
in a country/state where NovaTech has legal entity or EOR partnership.

Eligible countries (2026): United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, India,
Australia, Singapore.

2.2 Hybrid
Employee splits time between a NovaTech office and remote location. Minimum office
days: 0 (default). Teams may set local norms up to 3 days/week in office with
director approval.

2.3 Office-Based
Employee works primarily from assigned NovaTech office. Reserved for roles
requiring physical lab access, hardware testing, or executive leadership.

2.4 Temporary Remote
Approved for up to 30 consecutive days for personal circumstances (relocation,
family care, travel). Requires manager approval via WorkDay. Extensions beyond
30 days require HR approval.

================================================================================
3. ELIGIBILITY AND APPROVAL
================================================================================

3.1 Default Eligibility
All roles at Level 1–5 are remote-eligible unless listed in the "Office-Required
Roles" appendix (Appendix A). Currently office-required roles:
  - Data Center Operations Engineer
  - Hardware Lab Technician
  - Executive Assistant (C-Suite)
  - Office Manager

3.2 New Hires
Remote work status is confirmed in offer letter. Changes require 30-day notice
to manager and HR.

3.3 Relocation
Employees may relocate within their approved country without approval. Cross-border
relocation requires HR, Legal, and Tax review (4–6 week lead time). Compensation
may be adjusted to local market rates.

3.4 Performance Requirements
Remote employees must maintain "Meets Expectations" or higher on annual review.
Employees rated "Needs Improvement" may be required to work from office 3 days/week
for 90-day improvement period.

================================================================================
4. HOME OFFICE REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

4.1 Workspace Standards
Employees must maintain a dedicated, quiet workspace with:
  - Reliable internet: minimum 50 Mbps download, 10 Mbps upload
  - Backup connectivity option (mobile hotspot or secondary ISP)
  - Ergonomic chair and desk (stipend available — see 4.3)

4.2 Internet Reimbursement
Fully remote employees receive $75/month internet stipend, processed with payroll.
Hybrid employees (1+ office days/week): $40/month. Submit via ExpenseBot by the
5th of each month.

4.3 Home Office Setup Stipend
One-time $1,000 stipend for new remote hires. Covers desk, chair, monitor, or
accessories. Submit receipts within 60 days of start date. Existing employees
who transitioned to remote before 2024 are not eligible.

4.4 Co-Working Spaces
NovaTech partners with WeWork and Regus. Employees may expense up to $300/month
for co-working membership with manager pre-approval. Book through TravelPortal.

================================================================================
5. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

5.1 VPN
All remote access to internal systems requires NovaTech VPN (GlobalProtect).
Split tunneling is disabled. VPN must be active before accessing:
  - GitHub Enterprise repositories
  - AWS/GCP production environments
  - Customer data systems (CRM, support tickets with PII)

5.2 Device Security
Company-issued laptops must have:
  - FileVault/BitLocker encryption enabled
  - CrowdStrike Falcon endpoint protection (auto-installed)
  - Automatic OS updates within 7 days of release
  - Screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity

Personal devices (BYOD) are NOT permitted for accessing NovaTech systems except
via VDI (Virtual Desktop) for read-only Confluence access.

5.3 Physical Security
Employees must not leave company laptops visible in vehicles or public spaces.
Report lost or stolen devices within 1 hour to security@novatech.io and IT
Service Desk. Remote wipe will be initiated immediately.

5.4 Confidentiality
Remote employees must ensure household members and visitors cannot view or hear
confidential information during work hours. Use privacy screens in shared spaces.
Customer calls must be taken in private rooms.

5.5 Clean Desk Policy (Remote)
At end of each workday, secure physical documents in locked storage. Shred
confidential papers using cross-cut shredder (reimbursable up to $50).

================================================================================
6. COMMUNICATION AND AVAILABILITY
================================================================================

6.1 Core Hours
Remote employees must be reachable via Slack during core collaboration hours
(10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time). Slack status must reflect availability accurately.

6.2 Response Times
  - Slack messages: within 2 hours during core hours
  - Email: within 24 hours on business days
  - PagerDuty alerts (on-call): within 15 minutes, 24/7

6.3 Video Meetings
Camera-on is expected for team meetings, 1:1s, and client calls. Camera-off
permitted for all-hands and training sessions. Background blur or NovaTech
virtual background required when home environment is visible.

6.4 Time Zone Management
Employees must display their time zone in Slack profile. Schedule meetings across
time zones using Clockwise or World Time Buddy. No meetings before 8:00 AM or
after 7:00 PM in any participant's local time without consent.

================================================================================
7. REMOTE ONBOARDING
================================================================================

7.1 Equipment Shipping
Laptops ship 5 business days before start date to home address. Track via IT
portal. Report shipping issues to onboarding@novatech.io within 24 hours.

7.2 Virtual Orientation
Week 1: Company orientation (Day 1), team intro (Day 2), security training
(Day 3), tools setup (Day 4), buddy check-in (Day 5).

7.3 First 90 Days
Remote new hires have weekly 1:1 with manager, bi-weekly buddy sync, and 30/60/90
day check-ins with HR Business Partner.

================================================================================
8. EXPENSES AND TRAVEL
================================================================================

8.1 Office Visits
When visiting a NovaTech office, expense train/flight and hotel per Travel
Policy (TRV-2026-001). No per-diem for day trips under 50 miles.

8.2 Team Offsites
Company-sponsored team offsites: 1 per team per year, budget $1,500/person.
Organized by team lead with Finance pre-approval.

8.3 Quarterly Office Days
Optional "Connection Days" at regional offices, typically first Thursday of each
quarter. Company provides lunch. Travel not reimbursed unless required by manager.

================================================================================
9. POLICY VIOLATIONS
================================================================================

Violations may result in:
  - First offense: Written warning and mandatory security retraining
  - Second offense: Required office attendance 3 days/week for 60 days
  - Third offense: Termination of remote privilege or employment

Critical violations (data breach, VPN bypass, sharing credentials): immediate
termination regardless of offense count.

================================================================================
10. POLICY UPDATES
================================================================================

HR reviews this policy annually. Changes communicated via company all-hands and
email 30 days before effective date.
